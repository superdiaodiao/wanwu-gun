What reads from the fixed tee camera (survey + try shots, shots/try2*.png):
- an EARTH FACE turned to the camera: a level that is higher and farther, with water (not green) in front of it
  (hole 28's terrace, the U's top bar across the pen/gap, the staircase's left edge);
- a face turned towards the middle of the screen (the inside of an L/U gap), e.g. a raised tee leg's side;
- a deep hollow round the cup: its far side faces the camera, chevrons point into it.

ball (downhill to the cup, a hollow round the cup, a rail behind a raised cup) or sits where a firm putt is
the natural one anyway. No engine change needed: the ramp/hill shapes plus not-green gaps give the faces.
